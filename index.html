Zigline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Zigline handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Zigline does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The following data may be collected automatically through integrated third-party services:

  • Usage and performance data: in-game interactions, session length, launch/crash logs, and general device information (device model, operating system version, language, approximate region).
  • Identifiers: device and advertising identifiers used for analytics and advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics and performance analysis. Provided by Google LLC (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Unity Cloud Diagnostics — crash and error reporting. Provided by Unity Technologies (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising (to be enabled in future versions). When enabled, it may use the advertising identifier to serve ads.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to operate the game, fix bugs, measure performance, improve the experience, and (when enabled) show ads. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Zigline is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Data is processed under the security standards of the respective third-party providers and retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.

Your Rights

You can reset or turn off ad personalization from your device settings. Contact us for any requests regarding your data.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
